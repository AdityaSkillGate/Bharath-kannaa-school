Foundational Trust

Shri Bharath Kanna Educational & Charitable Trust

Founded under the Indian Trusts Act as a non-profit registered charitable body, the Trust has dedicated itself for over 25 years to establishing world-class educational institutions.

The Trust firmly believes that education is a sacred civic duty, not a commercial enterprise. Every surplus rupee generated is systematically re-invested into campus modernization, merit-cum-means scholarships for underprivileged rural students, teacher welfare endowments, and state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure.

Guided by ethical governance and statutory transparency, the board conducts quarterly operational reviews ensuring complete adherence to national educational policies and state regulatory norms.
