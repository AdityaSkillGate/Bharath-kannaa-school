Our Genesis & Legacy

A Quarter Century of Unwavering Commitment to Youth

Established in 1998 by visionaries devoted to education, Shri Bharath Kanna Matriculation Higher Secondary School has grown from a humble institution into one of the state's most revered centers of scholastic achievement.

Situated in an expansive, verdant campus, the school was created with a clear objective: to bring metropolitan standards of academic quality, science laboratory infrastructure, and modern technology to our region while keeping tuition fees accessible to every aspiring family.

Over the past 25 years, our alumni have distinguished themselves as doctors, software architects, civil servants, entrepreneurs, and researchers globally. Today, with more than 2,500 students and 120 dedicated teachers, we continue to uphold that sacred commitment.
