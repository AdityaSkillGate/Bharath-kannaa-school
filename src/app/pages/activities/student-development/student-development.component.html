Student Governance

Democratic Student Council & House System

Leadership is not taught by lecture; it is cultivated through active stewardship, democratic responsibility, and collective teamwork.

Every academic year, students democratically elect the School Head Boy, Head Girl, Sports Captain, Cultural Secretary, and House Prefects across our four spirited houses: Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, and Topaz.

Prefects lead morning assemblies, organize intra-school literary fests, maintain corridor discipline, and act as empathetic mediators for peer conflict resolution.
