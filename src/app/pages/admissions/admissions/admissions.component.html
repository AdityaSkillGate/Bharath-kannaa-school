Fee Structure & Guidelines

In strict compliance with the Tamil Nadu Private Schools Fee Determination Committee, our fees are transparent, highly competitive, and all-inclusive of standard lab facilities, sports equipment, and library resources.

Term Payment Flexibility:

Tuition fees may be settled in 3 equal term installments across June, October, and January.

Merit Scholarships:

Students scoring 95%+ in Grade 10 Board examinations receive up to 50% tuition concession for Senior Secondary streams.

Digital & Cashless Modes:

Safe UPI, Net Banking, and parent mobile portal payment options available with instantaneous digital receipts.
